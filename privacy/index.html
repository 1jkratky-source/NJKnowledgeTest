Privacy policy

The practice tool is designed to work without an account and to keep learning progress in your browser.

Information stored on your device

The site uses browser local storage to remember practice attempts, correct answers, missed-question counts, recent activity, completed mock exams, and the best mock-exam score. This data remains in the browser you used. Selecting “Reset progress” removes that learning record from local storage.

Accounts and forms

The site currently has no user accounts, email signup, contact form, payment flow, or cross-device progress service. Do not submit personal information through browser developer tools or URL parameters.

Hosting logs

The site is hosted by Netlify. Like most web hosts, Netlify may process technical request information such as IP address, browser information, requested pages, timestamps, and security events to deliver and protect the service. Netlify's own privacy terms govern that processing.

Advertising and analytics

No advertising or third-party analytics service is currently integrated. If advertising or analytics is added, this policy and any required consent controls will be updated before activation. Advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies where permitted and disclosed.

External links

Answer explanations and study pages link to NJ MVC, other government sources, and selected references. Those sites have their own privacy practices.

Changes

The effective date at the top of this page will change when the policy is materially updated.
