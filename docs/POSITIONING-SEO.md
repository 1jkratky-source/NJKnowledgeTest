# Product Positioning and Search Strategy

## Positioning

**The free, NJ-specific learning system for passing the MVC knowledge test with confidence.**

Unlike generic national quizzes relabeled for New Jersey, NJ Permit Practice focuses on the state's distinctive GDL rules, penalties, distances, alcohol laws, and test procedures. Unlike large banks that optimize mainly for question count, it samples every major subject, explains every answer, cites the governing source, and keeps the complete learning loop free without an account.

## Primary promise

Start with a balanced 10-question diagnostic, study the weak categories it reveals, review missed questions, and prove readiness with a realistic 50-question mock exam requiring 40 correct.

## Audience

1. New Jersey high-school students preparing for a first permit test.
2. Adults getting a first New Jersey license.
3. Drivers transferring a foreign license who must pass the knowledge test.
4. Parents, teachers, and driving instructors looking for a free current resource.

## Search and answer-engine principles

- Put the practice experience first; supporting content begins below it.
- Answer factual questions in the first sentence, then provide context and an official source.
- Keep claims specific and verifiable. Never claim access to “real” or leaked live-test questions.
- Maintain visible reviewed dates and an editorial policy.
- Keep important factual content in static HTML rather than relying only on client-side rendering.
- Use one canonical URL per page, descriptive titles, internal links, structured data, a sitemap, and `llms.txt`.
- Update page text, structured data, sitemap dates, and `llms.txt` together when rules change.

## Monetization path

The product should earn trust and repeat use before adding ads. When advertising begins:

1. Place ads between substantial educational sections or in desktop side rails.
2. Never insert ads between a question and its choices, between an answer and its explanation, or inside navigation controls.
3. Reserve enough fixed space to prevent layout shift.
4. Keep the entire bank free and avoid fake download or “next question” controls.
5. Add the ad provider and consent behavior to the privacy policy before activation.
6. Publish the provider-issued `ads.txt` entry only after an account and publisher ID exist.

## Measurement plan

- Search impressions and clicks by query cluster in Google Search Console.
- Landing-page engagement for practice, study guide, and test-details pages.
- Quick 10 completion rate.
- Percentage of Quick 10 users who start category study or a mock exam.
- Mock-exam completion and repeat-attempt rate.
- Returning visitors and missed-question review usage.

Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools should be configured after deployment. Their verification tokens must be added only after the accounts supply them.
