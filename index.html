Direct answers

NJ permit test FAQ

These answers describe the basic automobile knowledge test, not the separate CDL or motorcycle exams.

How many questions are on the New Jersey permit test?

There are 50 scored multiple-choice questions, plus an organ-donation survey question. You may miss up to 10 scored questions and still pass.

What score do I need to pass?

You need 40 correct answers out of 50, which is 80%. This site's full mock exam uses the same 40-of-50 target.

Is all of the practice free?

Yes. The complete 334-question bank, explanations, category study, missed-question review, and unlimited mock exams are free with no signup.

Are these leaked questions from the real test?

No. The questions are original study material built from the public NJ Driver Manual, MVC sample material, official rules, and recurring subjects reported by students. The secure live test bank is not public.

What should I study most?

Do not study road signs alone. NJ-specific GDL restrictions, alcohol rules, points and penalties, parking distances, right-of-way, vehicle control, and sharing the road are all important. Use Quick 10 to find a weak category, then study that category directly.

What happens if I fail the knowledge test?

NJ MVC says you can retest after seven days. Review your missed questions before starting another full mock exam.

Do I need an appointment?

Yes. The MVC directs applicants to purchase an examination permit and schedule a knowledge-test appointment. A knowledge test may be available during the permit appointment at some locations.

Is this an official NJ MVC website?

No. NJ Permit Practice is an independent educational resource and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the New Jersey Motor Vehicle Commission.
