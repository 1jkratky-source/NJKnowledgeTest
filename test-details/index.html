NJ knowledge test: what to expect

The essential details for the basic New Jersey automobile knowledge test, with links to the official MVC pages that control each requirement.

How many questions are on the NJ permit test?

50 scored questions, with 40 correct required to pass.The MVC also includes an organ-donation survey question.

The passing score is 80%. The public MVC sample test confirms both the 50-question length and the 40-correct threshold. This site uses that same scoring rule for full mock exams.

How much does the NJ examination permit cost?

The automobile examination permit fee is $10.The MVC lists separate fees for motorcycle and commercial permits.

Check the official MVC license and permit fee schedule before your appointment because fees can change.

Do you need an appointment?

Yes. MVC licensing centers handle first-time permits and knowledge testing by appointment. The usual sequence is:

  1. Make a first-time permit appointment and bring the required identity and residency documents.
  2. Purchase the examination permit.
  3. Take the vision screening and knowledge test. If testing is unavailable during the permit appointment, schedule a separate knowledge-test appointment.
  4. After passing, have the permit validated and continue with the supervised-driving requirements that apply to your age and permit route.

Start from the NJ MVC first driver license page; do not rely on appointment links shared by unofficial websites.

What should you bring?

The MVC instructs first-time applicants to bring the documents needed for its identity-verification process, including proof of identity, New Jersey residency, and Social Security number or an accepted alternative. Requirements depend on the credential and applicant.

Use the MVC's current document selector and appointment instructions rather than a static third-party checklist. Bring the permit and required documents again when the MVC instructions tell you to do so.

What happens if you fail?

You may retake the knowledge test after seven days.The MVC's first-license instructions state the seven-day waiting period.

There is no benefit to repeating the same memorized quiz during that week. Review the categories you missed and then take a fresh balanced mock exam.

What languages are available?

NJ MVC currently lists written tests in English, Arabic, Chinese (Mandarin), French, Spanish, Korean, Polish, Portuguese, Russian, Japanese, Hindi, Albanian, and Turkish. Oral tests are offered in English and Spanish.

If the knowledge test is unavailable in your language, MVC says you may request an interpreter through its state contract during the permit appointment. Interpreter appointments typically require additional lead time. Confirm details on the official knowledge-test page.

Is the NJ knowledge test timed?

The public MVC pages reviewed for this guide do not publish a standard time limit for the basic automobile knowledge test. Prepare for accuracy rather than relying on a rumored timer, and ask staff about accommodations or testing procedures at your location.

What subjects are tested?

The official driver manual covers licensing and permits, vehicle operation, signs and signals, right-of-way, speed and parking, defensive driving, emergencies, alcohol and penalties, sharing the road, and vehicle requirements. The secure live question mix is not public.
